❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order a product?

Tap “Add to Cart”, open your cart and choose “Order on WhatsApp”. The message will include your selected product names and totals.

How will I receive my files?

Confirm payment and delivery details with the seller on WhatsApp. This demo does not automatically deliver paid files.

Can I pay online here?

No payment gateway is connected in this static version. Arrange payment directly with the seller until a payment provider and backend are set up.

Do image previews upload publicly?

No. They only preview locally in your browser. Shared product uploads require a server or cloud storage setup.
